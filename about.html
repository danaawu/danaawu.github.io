About Me

✦

Hi! I'm Dana, a student at UNC-Chapel Hill studying advertising and public relations alongside public policy. I've always been interested in understanding people's experiences and the stories that make them who they are. Whether I'm working with photography, video or writing, I love finding creative ways to share stories that might otherwise go unheard. Beyond storytelling, I also have a love for fashion and the creativity that comes with it. I see fashion as another form of self-expression, where even the smallest details can communicate something about someone's personality. I enjoy exploring different styles and finding inspiration in the things around me.
