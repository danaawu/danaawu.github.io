My Work

✦

What I enjoy most about creative work is the process of turning an idea into something people can see, experience or connect with. I love brainstorming new concepts, experimenting with photography and designing visuals that bring my ideas to life. I'm especially interested in copywriting and creative direction because they combine so many of the things I enjoy: storytelling, design and finding unique ways to communicate a message. Whether I'm behind a camera or developing a creative concept, I love having the freedom to experiment and make something my own.
